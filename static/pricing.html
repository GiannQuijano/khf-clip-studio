What Clip Studio costs to run

There's no software licence. The foundation pays only for the server that runs the studio and a few cents of AI per video. Most months come to about $30 to $40.

$27.50per month, fixed (server + storage)
≈ 10¢per video for Claude's clip picks and captions

How the AI cost is worked out: Claude Opus 5.5 is billed at $4 per million word pieces ("tokens") read and $20 per million written. A 10-minute talk is roughly 4,000 tokens in and 2,000 to 5,000 out, which comes to 6 to 12 cents. Each "Find new clips" costs about the same again. A 1-hour recording is about 20 cents.

How the foundation gets value from it

More posts from every trip

One 10-minute site visit can become 3 to 5 ready-to-post clips with captions, instead of one long video nobody finishes.

Hours back each week

Finding the moment, cutting, reframing to vertical and typing subtitles usually takes 30 to 60 minutes per clip by hand. Here it's a few clicks and a review.

Cheaper than outsourcing

If an editor charges $20 per short clip, just two clips a month cover the cost of the studio.

Consistent and on-brand

Every clip uses the foundation's teal-and-navy captions and the same donor-focused voice, whoever on the team makes it.

Months you don't use it

The server is billed while it's running, whether or not anyone uploads a video, so an unused month still costs about $27.50. Claude is pay-as-you-go, so it costs nothing in a month with no videos.

If you expect a long break, you can pause the server. In dashboard.render.com, open the khf-clip-studio service, go to Settings and choose Suspend. Render bills the server by the second while it runs, so a suspended server should stop the $25 charge; the storage disk ($2.50/month) may still be billed so your videos stay saved. Check Billing a day later to confirm. Choose Resume when you need it again; it's back in a few minutes.

Moving the billing to the foundation

The studio is currently paid for on a temporary card. To move it over:

  1. Render (server and storage)5 min
    Go to dashboard.render.com and sign in. (First, Giann invites your email to the Render workspace with the Admin role, which can change the payment card. Accept the invite from your inbox.) Then click Billing in the left-hand menu (or open the workspace menu in the top-left corner and choose Billing) and replace the payment card with the foundation's. To hand over the account entirely, invite the foundation's email under Workspace settings → Members, give it the Admin role, then remove the temporary account.
  2. Anthropic (Claude AI)10 min
    The foundation creates its own account at console.anthropic.com, adds a card or prepaid credit under Billing, and creates an API key under API keys. It's a good idea to set a monthly spend limit there too, such as $20.
  3. Swap the key2 min
    In Render, open the khf-clip-studio service → Environment, replace the value of ANTHROPIC_API_KEY with the foundation's key, and click Save. The studio restarts in a minute or two, and no videos are lost.
