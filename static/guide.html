How Clip Studio works

Clip Studio turns the videos you record in the field into short clips for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and LinkedIn. You upload a video, Claude finds the strongest moments and writes the captions, and you export clips that are ready to post.

Upload→Transcribe→Claude picks clips→Review & tweak→Export & post

Before your first video: set your brand voice

Click Brand settings in the top right. It's already filled in with Kind Human Foundation's mission, vision, tone, tagline and colors. Claude reads all of it before it picks clips and writes copy, so the more specific it is, the more on-brand the results. You can edit it any time:

  • Mission, vision, audience and tone. Claude uses these for what to pick and how to write.
  • Call to action and links. Paste your exact donate link here, for example "Donate: https://…". Claude only ever uses links from this box, so it never makes one up.
  • Avoid and hashtags. Words or styles to stay away from, and hashtags you want used when they fit.
  • Anything else. A current campaign, an event, or how to spell a place or a partner's name.
  • Brand colors. Used for the highlighted caption word and the on-screen title boxes.
  • Clip length and how many clips per video. The default is 15 to 60 seconds and up to 5 clips.

Settings apply to every new video, every "Find new clips" and every export.

Step by step

1

Upload a video

Drag a video onto Upload a video on the left, or click it to choose a file. Phone videos (.mov, .mp4) work as they are. Vertical or horizontal are both fine.

2

Wait while it's processed

The progress bar shows each stage. First, every word spoken is written out with its exact timing. Then Claude reads the transcript and picks the clips. As a rough guide, a 1-minute video takes 1 to 2 minutes and a 10-minute video about 5 to 10 minutes. You can leave the page and come back; your videos are listed on the left.

3

Review Claude's suggested clips

Each suggestion is a card with:

ScoreHow likely the clip is to perform well, out of 100. Best clips are listed first. HookThe opening line that grabs attention in the first seconds. Why it worksA short note on why this moment stands on its own. Context checkA warning when a clip could confuse or mislead without more context, or when something needs a check before posting (for example, a child's name). Caption & hashtagsReady to paste into your post. Click Copy. First commentA comment to post under your own post right after publishing: the donate link when the clip asks people to give, or a key number or one-line summary when it tells a story. Suggested thumbnailA cover image, framed like your export, with an idea for cover text. Claude picks the moment from what's said, and the studio picks the clearest frame around it. Prefer another moment? Pause the player on it and click Use the frame in the player. Click Download thumbnail to save it. Shorts titleA title for YouTube Shorts.

Click ▶ Preview to play just that clip in the player above.

4

Want different clips? Tell Claude what to look for

Type an instruction in the box above the clips and press Find new clips. For example:

  • "Moments about the kids at the well"
  • "A 15-second teaser for TikTok"
  • "Thank-you moments for our donors"
5

Fine-tune a clip

Start / EndNudge the times (in seconds) to trim. Preview again to check. Format9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. 4:5 or 1:1 for feed posts. 16:9 for YouTube and LinkedIn. FramingFill crops to fill the frame (best for one person talking). Fit keeps the whole shot over a blurred background (best for wide scenes). Crop positionWith Fill, slide left or right to keep the subject centred. CaptionsWith Add captions to the video ticked, word-by-word captions are drawn into the picture itself, so they show on every platform, even with the sound off (most people scroll muted). Untick it for a clip with no captions. Brand highlights each word in your accent color. On-screen titleA headline shown for the first 4 seconds. Leave it empty for none. Title style & positionPick a box in your primary or accent color, a black box, or white text with no box, and put the title at the top, middle or bottom. At the bottom, captions move up while the title shows so they never overlap.
6

Export and download

Click Export clip. The finished video appears in the Exports tab after a minute or two. Click Download to save the .mp4, then upload it to your social platform with the caption you copied.

7

Cut your own clip from the transcript

Open Transcript & manual cut. Click a first and a last line to select that range, or play the video and use Set start here and Set end here. The same export options appear below. You can also download the full transcript as an .srt subtitle file.

Tips for videos that clip well

Sound first

Clear speech matters more than picture. Get close to the speaker, avoid wind, and use a clip-on mic if you have one.

Film vertical when you can

Most clips end up 9:16. Filming vertical means nothing important gets cropped out.

Say the context out loud

"We're in Proddatur, standing at the new borewell" makes a clip understandable on its own. Name places, numbers and who you're thanking.

Always review before posting

Claude is a strong first editor, not the final one. Watch each export and read the context check before you post.

Questions

Who can see our videos?

Videos and exports are stored on the foundation's own server. To pick clips, the transcript text (not the video) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API. The studio doesn't have its own login yet, so only share the link with your team.

How big can a video be?

Phone videos up to about 30 minutes work well. Very large 4K files upload slowly; if you can, export or record at 1080p.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. Speech is detected automatically in most major languages, and captions are written in the language spoken.

Something went wrong. What do I do?

Open the video and click Try again. If it fails again, send a screenshot of the error to your Clip Studio contact.

Can I rename a video?

Yes. Open it and click Rename next to its name. The new name shows in the list on the left.

How do I free up space?

Open a video and click Delete video. This removes the original and all its exports from the server.
